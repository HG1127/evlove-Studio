# Zero-reference calibration contrasts

The two-order real paragraph-loss diagnostic on `399abac` remains failed: its structural threshold changes with order, and its pairwise improvement delta changes from -10 to +15. Both responses and the previous oracle remain sealed. Offline replay through Harness settlement and Core Winner selection preserves the integrity failure, marks both inconsistent criteria unverified, and blocks promotion. This is a diagnostic case, not an accepted evolved artifact.

Calibration v1 requires every criterion to exhibit a negative Candidate-minus-Original delta in a labelled regression control. This cannot qualify a benefit-only relative criterion when its Original self-comparison is correctly zero: all scores are integers from zero through 100, so none can be at least one point below that reference. A worse artifact can have zero benefit without a negative score. Inflating the Original score or permitting a negative score would change the measurement rather than solve this problem.

The opt-in `judge-calibration-plan-v2` adds the independently registered expectation `no-improvement`. It is restricted to a non-protected pairwise outcome criterion with a positive unchanged threshold in a known-regression control. Both orders must report an exact zero score and `pass:false` for that Candidate. Every Original self-comparison for this criterion, including positive and identical controls, must also be exactly zero and failing. Partial positive scores below the threshold do not satisfy this expectation. A separately labelled positive control must still improve by the registered minimum, pass the original absolute threshold, and have Safety PASS. Ordinary absolute semantic, integrity and protection criteria retain their degradation requirements.

This additional oracle interpretation has an explicit contract version. V1 parsing and assessment retain their original behavior. Existing observations cannot be relabelled under v2; a future plan needs independent oracle approval and preregistration preceding its first request, with exact source/code/route/rubric/paper bindings and both orders. V2 additionally reads the exact approval artifact bytes through the existing injected text port, verifies their SHA and canonical evidence identity, validates their separate preregistration/oracle kinds, and checks that each binds the complete canonical plan and was recorded by registration. Only the two approval references are excluded from the plan digest to avoid circular hashes; control and anchor array order stays fixed. Reusing an older approved object's hash cannot approve a modified expectation or source. Trusted independence and preregistration verification remain mandatory: a caller-written approval JSON alone certifies nothing.

The method still rejects copy disagreement, threshold or direction conflicts, missing anchors, incomplete contrasts and unqualified application evidence. No Mission acceptance, model authority, new budget, hidden-paper change, score rewrite, retry or deployment follows from a calibration decision.

Tests exercise a synthetic attainable zero-reference plan, exhaustive v1 rejection of all 101 candidate scores against a zero reference, mixed absolute and relative criteria, false zero passes, partial benefits, order changes and trust failures. They establish the calibration method's logic; they do not establish that the current MiniMax route is calibrated or that a real SOP has evolved successfully.
