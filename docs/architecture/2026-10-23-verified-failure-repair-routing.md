The rejection is actionable without converting the incomplete criterion into a product failure or success. Gate F retains its unverified overall state, checks the physical frozen files, and may target only the verified failed criteria. An otherwise undecided assessment still waits for evidence. Missing physical files, changed bindings, duplicate results, and independent final-paper rejection never authorize this repair shortcut.

Passing observations retain the existing freshness rules.
