Date: 2026-10-27. Status: implemented; local verification. The previous real trial remains terminal and unaccepted.

## Decision

`experiment-visible-repair-v2` is a new explicit policy opt-in. Existing absent/disabled/v1 policies keep their behavior. After all original minimums pass, v2 may repair a non-protected target that has a strictly lower verified score than its paired, passing Baseline score on the same Visible cases. The target must have been declared by the Candidate rationale. Core recomputes Winner eligibility; an already eligible Candidate does not qualify for this route. Missing, demo, ambiguous, duplicate or unpaired evidence cannot justify mutation. Equal scores without a measured regression do not qualify.

The repair record adds an optional, fingerprint-bound feedback classification while retaining v1 inline and v2-reference history compatibility. Existing records without it keep their original absolute-failure meaning. The AI receives the unchanged current/baseline/minimum scores and explicit notice that the absolute criterion still passes. No PASS flag or minimum is rewritten. Only the verified regressed targets may change; existing protected scope, full failure archive, durable pre-call claim, shared repair budget and original time window remain binding.

The repaired file still requires a new physical identity and complete Visible → ordinary Hidden → unchanged independent final → Gate F validation. Repair cannot promote a Candidate or reuse its parent's scores. Existing terminal Missions cannot be reopened by installing or opting into this policy.

Winner's existing rule is unchanged: improvement of at least one point globally, or at least one point on the targeted criteria with no substantive global regression, or fewer failed hard gates. Campaign `minImprovement=0.5` is a separate round-progress/stopping parameter, not this Winner threshold. Earlier commentary confused the two; the fourth trial's rejected decision remains correct under the original rule.
