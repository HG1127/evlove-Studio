# Explicit relative outcome reference and unknown evidence

The V2/V3 calibration checker already requires exact Original zero/false scores for a registered non-improvement pairwise outcome criterion. Generic zero-change instructions did not explain that relative improvement construct separately from standalone quality. Public metadata alone must not silently activate a new scale or reinterpret past observations.

`EVOLUTION_AI_PAIRED_OUTCOME_REFERENCE=zero-v1` is an explicit server-only, prospective policy for both ordinary paired Missions and calibration. Unset retains the previous instructions and scoring-route fingerprint. Invalid values stop paired judging without echoing the value. Only public pairwise/outcome/non-protected criteria with a positive unchanged minimum are eligible. Artifact-specific, absolute and protected criteria keep their meanings and minima. Both mode and policy version enter the scoring-route fingerprint, preventing recovery of observations from a different policy.

The new instruction identifies slot C as the public reference anchor. Its self-comparison and identical copies are zero/false only for eligible relative criteria. A different subject is zero/false only when sufficient comparable evidence confirms non-improvement or regression. Eligible rows carry structured `relativeEvidenceStatus: sufficient | unknown`; missing, conflicting or incomparable evidence requires unknown and gaps. The independent state permits an unknown relative comparison to coexist with a known global Safety FAIL. No gold labels, control kinds, predicted scores or preferred subject are added. This is partial blinding with a public anchor, not complete blinding. Full-confirmation admission additionally requires the frozen V3 plan to declare the corresponding existing no-improvement controls and every observation to record the configured policy. V2 read compatibility remains; V1 is never activated from metadata.

An optional, strict `outcomeReferencePolicy` receipt field records the server-owned opt-in; the model cannot supply it. Per-criterion evidence status survives response parsing, hashing, journal recovery and receipt summarization. The explicitly selected JSONL grammar adds that field; the default grammar remains unchanged. Generation guidance requests the field, but missing status is valid unqualified data, never an excuse to correct or retry an adverse verdict. Under the active policy, all subjects/copies in both orders must have sufficient status; unknown or missing status, or global REVIEW, makes eligible relative scores unverified during ordinary settlement and paired promotion checks. Unrelated evidence-backed absolute failures remain intact. Independently, registered V2/V3 zero-reference calibration cannot qualify REVIEW as a known zero failure, including older receipts without the new field. Known evidence-backed Safety FAIL negatives with sufficient relative evidence remain valid calibration data. Valid REVIEW, negative and incorrect reference scores are preserved for qualification rather than rejected as malformed, clamped or retried.

The pure eligibility, registered activation and qualification live in Core; schemas live in Contracts; Harness keeps receipt-to-request identity checks; environment selection, instructions and admission compose in the backend. No provider, credential, deployment or persistent environment is changed by this patch. Tests use synthetic labels/provider responses and prove protocol behavior only; they do not establish real calibration, a successful Candidate, business benefit or Lesson transfer.
