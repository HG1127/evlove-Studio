## Decision

Harness projects validated native observations into a bounded metadata section of the existing measured-document context. It carries source and Original/subject hashes, actual case identities, profile/kernel identities, measured control counts/results, retention facts and computed-style comparison counts. It preserves complete observed failures as well as passes. Duplicate case identities, incomplete/errored observations, invalid hashes and mismatches against trusted frozen Candidate bindings cannot strengthen this context.

The projection reads no new artifacts. It excludes raw HTML/CSS/JS, producer prose, arbitrary extra fields, query text and style-group contents. Case-independent duplicate physical reports share one fact group while retaining their actual observed case IDs. Native facts have a separate 24,000-character budget with explicit omissions. They remain physical observations, not semantic, pixel-visual, business or Criterion verdicts.

Candidate, current-version and initial Baseline review use the same fact presentation. Policy marker `server-measured-document-review-v3-native-facts` participates in both the Baseline measurement fingerprint and the AI scoring-route identity. Legacy data remains stored; old scores cannot silently share the new assessment route or current Holdout-recovery marker. No acceptance threshold, test case, coverage gate, native observation, frozen file or historical verdict is rewritten.
