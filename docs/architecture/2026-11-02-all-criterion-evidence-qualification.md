# Explicit evidence status for every paired criterion

The first explicit relative-reference policy retained relative unknown independently from global Safety FAIL. Independent preparation review then reproduced the same gap for an absolute criterion: a missing-evidence zero/false placeholder could match a false oracle while a separate known failure required overall FAIL. Free-form gaps and one global Safety value cannot identify every unknown criterion.

The prospective explicit service policy now requests `evidenceStatus: sufficient | unknown` for every issued criterion in every slot. Sufficient means enough supplied evidence to support the particular verdict, including a confirmed failure; it does not mean passing or safe. Missing, conflicting or incomparable proof is unknown independently of overall Safety. No criterion meaning, minimum, independent label or protected constraint changes.

The runtime stamps the strict receipt-only `evidenceStatusPolicy: criterion-evidence-v1` field, which the model cannot supply. Core requires sufficient status in both subjects and the repeated reference in both orders for each criterion. Unknown or absent status makes that criterion unverified while preserving other known failures and Safety FAIL. Explicit calibration also rejects overall Safety REVIEW. Valid missing/unknown state is unqualified data, never a format-correction or adverse-verdict retry.

The server's existing explicit `EVOLUTION_AI_PAIRED_OUTCOME_REFERENCE=zero-v1` selection enables this versioned combination of relative reference semantics and criterion evidence qualification for ordinary Missions and calibration. Mode, instruction version and evidence policy enter the scoring-route identity. Full admission additionally requires matching evidence-policy markers on every observation. The separately selected generation schema and JSONL grammar expose the new field; default instructions/grammar and route remain unchanged. Earlier relative-only JSONL records, result fields and receipt qualification remain readable under their earlier policy and route, without inferring sufficient status.

This follows the package direction and preserves public schema validation, receipt-to-request identity and journal hash/recovery checks. It does not change provider configuration, deploy, register a trial or spend model budget. Synthetic protocol tests and independent review cannot replace real calibration, an accepted delivered Candidate, business tasks or causal Lesson transfer.
